## 1. Core Contractual Provisions

The SkillConnect Terms of Service govern all access to the web platform, mobile interfaces, and booking facilitation services.

### 1.1 Marketplace Intermediary Status
* **Platform Role**: SkillConnect operates exclusively as an online communications and transaction marketplace connecting independent consumers with independent trade contractors.
* **No Direct Employer Relationship**: Service professionals are independent third-party business operators, not employees, agents, or joint-venturers of SkillConnect.
* **Limitation of Liability**: SkillConnect's total aggregate liability for platform defects is limited to the greater of $100 or the platform commission fees collected on the applicable booking.

### 1.2 User Eligibility & Accounts
* Users must be at least 18 years of age and capable of entering into legally binding contracts.
* Users are responsible for safeguarding login credentials and promptly reporting unauthorized access.

### 1.3 Fee Structures & Payment Authorization
* Customers explicitly authorize the platform to place pre-authorization holds on their payment method upon booking, and to capture funds (diagnostic fee + labor + approved materials) upon job completion sign-off or 48-hour expiration.

### 1.4 Dispute Resolution & Mandatory Arbitration
* All individual disputes arising from platform use are subject to binding individual arbitration under the American Arbitration Association (AAA) commercial rules.
* Users waive any right to initiate or participate in class action litigation against SkillConnect.
