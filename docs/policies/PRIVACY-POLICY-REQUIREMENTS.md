## 1. Required Privacy Disclosures

The SkillConnect Privacy Policy informs users regarding how personal data is collected, processed, protected, and shared across marketplace services.

### 1.1 Categories of Data Collected
1. **Directly Provided Information**: Name, email, mobile phone number, physical home address, uploaded issue photographs, and trade credentials.
2. **Automatically Collected Information**: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, referral URLs, and approximate geolocation derived from IP.
3. **Transactional Information**: Detailed booking history, scheduled appointment timestamps, review ratings, dispute communications, and escrow payout records.

### 1.2 Third-Party Service Disclosures
SkillConnect shares data strictly with vetted operational sub-processors:
* **Stripe, Inc.**: Payment tokenization, identity verification, 1099 tax filing.
* **Twilio Inc.**: SMS appointment alerts and masked proxy calling.
* **Resend**: Transactional email dispatch.
* **Persona Identities, Inc.**: Biometric liveness check and driver's license fraud verification.
* **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**: Cloud database hosting and encrypted S3 object storage.

### 1.3 Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / GDPR)
* Users have the right to request access to all collected personal data, demand correction of inaccuracies, and request total deletion ("Right to be Forgotten").
* SkillConnect **does not sell or share** user personal data with data brokers or behavioral advertising networks for commercial profit.
