## 1. Professional Partner Code of Conduct & Terms

All service professionals operating on SkillConnect contractually agree to the **Professional Service Agreement**:

### 1.1 Independent Contractor Status
* Professionals operate as independent business entities (sole proprietorships, LLCs, or corporations).
* SkillConnect does not set daily working hours, mandate uniforms, or direct the physical methods of trade craftsmanship.
* Professionals supply their own tools, vehicles, equipment, and materials.
* Tax compliance: SkillConnect issues IRS Form 1099-K / 1099-NEC annually via Stripe Connect.

### 1.2 Professional Standards & On-Site Conduct
* **Punctuality**: Technicians must notify customers via app if arrival will be delayed by more than 15 minutes.
* **Safety & PPE**: Appropriate personal protective equipment (safety goggles, insulated gloves, steel-toed boots) must be utilized during work.
* **Worksite Cleanliness**: Technicians must leave the work area in a broom-clean condition and remove all trade debris.
* **Anti-Disintermediation**: Technicians shall not solicit customers for off-platform cash transactions. Solicitations result in immediate permanent account termination.

---

## 2. Customer Responsibilities & On-Site Terms

Homeowners and business customers agree to provide:
* **Safe Working Environment**: Free from aggressive pets, biohazards, or verbal/physical harassment.
* **Adult on Premises**: An authorized adult (>= 18 years old) must be physically present during the entire appointment duration.
* **Accurate Descriptions**: Honest descriptions of the repair scope and any known pre-existing structural issues.
