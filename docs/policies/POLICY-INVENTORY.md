## 2. Legal Disclaimers & Review Mandate

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These policy documents establish technical and business requirements for drafting official agreements. They do not constitute formal legal counsel. Prior to commercial public launch in any state or national jurisdiction, all policies must be reviewed and countersigned by licensed practicing attorneys specializing in marketplace law, commercial contracts, and employment classification.
