## 1. Zero Tolerance Safety Standards

SkillConnect enforces an uncompromising zero-tolerance policy regarding the physical safety, dignity, and rights of both customers and service professionals:

### 1.1 Strictly Prohibited Conduct
* **Violence & Threats**: Physical intimidation, verbal harassment, discriminatory language, or weapons brandishing.
* **Sexual Harassment**: Unsolicited comments, inappropriate advances, or intrusive behavior.
* **Fraud & Impersonation**: Sharing technician accounts, submitting fabricated trade licenses, or creating dummy reviews.
* **Substance Use**: Operating while impaired by alcohol, narcotics, or prescription medication impacting safety.

### 1.2 Prohibited Services
The platform shall never be used to request or offer:
* Unpermitted structural demolitions violating building codes.
* Uncertified asbestos, biohazard, or hazardous radioactive waste handling.
* Tampering with municipal utility meters (water/gas/electric bypass).
* Firearm installation or weapon modification.

---

## 2. Emergency Escalation Protocol

If an imminent safety hazard or crime occurs on-site:
1. **Immediate Call 911**: Users are instructed to contact emergency local law enforcement first.
2. **Platform Incident Button**: Tap the in-app "Emergency Incident" button to instantly freeze booking records, disable messaging, and alert the 24/7 Trust & Safety Rapid Response desk.
