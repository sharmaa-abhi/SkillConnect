## 1. Domain Terminology

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Service Professional ("Pro")** | A licensed, verified independent tradesperson or contractor (plumber, electrician, carpenter, etc.) offering local services via SkillConnect. |
| **Customer** | A residential homeowner, tenant, or business representative booking a service professional through the platform. |
| **Diagnostic Inspection Fee** | A standardized upfront fee ($49–$89) charged for the technician to travel on-site, inspect the issue, and provide a binding repair quote. Credited 100% toward repair if approved. |
| **Sample Estimate** | A non-binding preliminary price range generated by algorithms or AI scoping to give customers indicative costs prior to booking. |
| **Escrow Pre-Authorization** | A temporary financial hold placed on the customer's credit card upon booking confirmation, captured only upon mutual job sign-off. |
| **Take Rate** | The percentage of the transaction retained by SkillConnect as marketplace commission (typically 12.5% standard; 8.5% for Pro subscribers). |
| **Verified Badge** | A visual indicator awarded to technicians who have passed multi-tier identity, state trade license, and commercial liability insurance audits. |
| **Service Radius** | The geographic boundary (in kilometers or miles) within which a technician is willing to travel for service calls without extra travel surcharges. |
| **Disintermediation** | The illicit practice of customers and technicians conducting off-platform cash transactions to evade marketplace commission fees. |
| **Housing Society Partner** | A residential homeowners association (HOA) or commercial facility management entity utilizing SkillConnect B2B portal for bulk property maintenance. |
| **Reduced Motion** | A user accessibility preference (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`) signaling that all non-essential UI animations should be disabled or minimized. |
